Receiver Control
Adjust radio behaviour, detector gates, and gain protection without touching the FPGA image.

This page is the appliance control surface. Operational knobs live here with short explanations; raw internals stay on Diagnostics.

Radio
Immediate control

Set the current gain mode and manual gain. These are the fastest controls for how the frontend behaves right now.

Use manual gain when you want reproducible experiments. Use automatic modes only when you specifically want the RFIC deciding the operating point.
Detector
Runtime tuning

These gates shape how aggressively the FPGA claims candidates. Use them when comparing strict versus permissive behaviour without rebuilding a bitstream.

Quiet and SNR shifts change preamble permissiveness. Holdoff protects the decoder bank from being retriggered too aggressively on nearby activity.
Gain Guard
Operational safety net

The gain guard tries to keep the frontend out of trouble without giving away too much range. These thresholds decide what “too hot” and “calm enough” mean.

A lower hot threshold protects strong-signal handling sooner. A longer calm hold keeps the gain from bouncing upward too quickly after a noisy burst.
